Part I · Ocean Science

GHG Emissions by Sector

GHG emissions

~7% of global GHG emissions come from human activity in the oceans and coasts.

Ocean warming begins with emissions on land and at sea: it is the downstream consequence of greenhouse gas emissions across the global economy. While the shipping industry alone accounts for ~3% of global GHG emissions, several sectors that operate in the ocean add up to roughly 7% of the global total: trade, fishing, offshore oil and gas, and coastal tourism. Marine carbon release from bottom trawling is another significant, and often overlooked, source of carbon release from ocean-floor sediments.
